# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who contributes to AI Automation Toolkit.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Respect other contributors and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the repository's GitHub Issues or contact the project maintainer privately when appropriate.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as responsibly as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Thank you for helping keep AI Automation Toolkit welcoming and constructive.
